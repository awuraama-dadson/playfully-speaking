Good to know

Questions, answered

Will anything be shipped to me?

No. The Group Chat is a digital download. You get the printable files right after purchase and print them at home.

What do I need to play?

Regular paper or card stock, a printer and scissors. No drinking, no phones and no props needed.

How many people can play?

It's made for 3 to 10 players. Bigger groups can split into teams.

Is it explicit?

No. It's made for ages 18 and up, but nothing in it is explicit. Anyone can skip any card without questions.

Is there scoring?

Only if you want it. There's a printable scoreboard so you can crown a Group Chat Admin at the end of the night.

What occasions is it good for?

Game nights, girls' nights, birthdays, house parties, sleepovers, reunions, and mixed groups of old and new friends.

Can I share the file with friends?

The game is for personal use only, so please don't share or resell the file. Your friends can grab their own copy on Selar.
