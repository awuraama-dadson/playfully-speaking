No. Love Me, Dare Me is a digital download. You get instant access to the print-ready PDFs after purchase.

How spicy is it?

It's made for adults 18+, with flirty questions, kissing challenges and bold dares. Every dare is an invitation, never an obligation, and either of you can skip any card with no explanation needed.

A printer, cardstock and scissors. Print at 100% size and cut along the guides. No board or apps needed.
