Good to know

Questions, answered

Will anything be shipped to me?

No. 'Tis the Season to Be Silly is a digital download. You get the printable files right after purchase and print them at home.

What do I need to play?

A printer, paper and scissors. No props, no phones and no special knowledge needed.

Who is it for?

Teens and adults. It's family-friendly and nothing in it is explicit.

How many people can play?

Two or more, so it works for a small family night or a big party.

How do you win?

You don't have to, but if you want a winner: keep every card you complete. Whoever has the most cards at the end wins.

What occasions is it good for?

Christmas Day, Christmas Eve, family gatherings, office parties, end-of-year hangouts, church and youth groups, and nights in with friends or roommates.

Can I share the file with friends?

The game is for personal use only, so please don't share or resell the file. Your friends can grab their own copy on Selar.
