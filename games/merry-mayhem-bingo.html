Good to know

Questions, answered

Will anything be shipped to me?

No. Merry Mayhem Bingo is a digital download. You get the printable file straight after purchase and print it at home or at a print shop.

Which paper size should I choose?

Choose A4 if you're in Ghana, the UK, Europe, Africa, Australia or most other regions. Choose US Letter if you're in the USA or Canada. Always print at 100% / actual size.

Do I need a colour printer?

Not at all. The cards are designed to look great in black and white too.

How many people can play?

Anywhere from 3 to 30 players, ages 8 and up. With 30 unique cards, everyone gets their own.

Is it okay for a work party?

Yes. The Holiday Party edition is written for friends, roommates and office parties, and it's fully workplace-safe.

Can I share the file with friends?

The game is for personal use only, so please don't share or resell the file. Your friends can grab their own copy on Selar.
