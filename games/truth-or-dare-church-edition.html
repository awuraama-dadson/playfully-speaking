Good to know

Questions, answered

Will anything be shipped to me?

No. Truth or Dare: Church Edition is a digital download. You get instant access to the print-ready PDFs after purchase.

Is it appropriate for church?

Yes. It's clean, faith-friendly fun with no awkward or embarrassing questions. Think “Sing the chorus of any worship song like an opera singer.”

How many people can play?

From 3 to 30+ players, so it works for a small Bible study or a full youth fellowship.

What are the Wild cards?

Nine surprise cards hidden in the deck, like Hot Seat, Partner Up, Swap It and Free Pass. Keep a Free Pass to skip any Truth or Dare later.

Who is it for?

Youth fellowships and youth groups, church retreats and camps, game nights and church hangouts, and Bible study icebreakers.

What do I need to play?

A printer, cardstock or thick paper, and scissors. The cards are standard playing-card size, 63 × 88 mm.

Can I share the file?

The game is for personal use only, so please don't share or resell the file.
