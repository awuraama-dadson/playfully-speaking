How to play

Draw, answer & win

No board, no apps, no extra equipment. Just the cards.

1

Shuffle

Print and cut the cards, shuffle them and put them face down.

2

Draw & read

Take turns drawing a card and reading it aloud. A grown-up or big kid can help.

3

Answer or do it

Answer the question or do the challenge. Got it? Win 1 point. A friend can give a hint.

4

First to 5 wins

Or play as a team: answer 10 cards together and everybody wins.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Will anything be shipped to me?

No. Little Geniuses is a digital download. You get instant access to the print-ready PDFs after purchase.

What age is it for?

Kids aged 5 to 9. Younger players can team up with a grown-up or a big kid who reads the cards aloud.

What if my child gets an answer wrong?

No problem. Put the card at the bottom of the pile and try it again later. Hints are always welcome.

Is there an answer key?

Yes. Each card has a code in the corner, like B3 or A7, that matches the answer key for grown-ups. For Silly Sparks and creative answers, any real try counts.

Can I use it in my classroom?

Yes. It's made for family game nights, rainy days, classrooms and homeschool, birthday parties, car trips and quiet time.

Will it use a lot of ink?

No. The cards have a light, ink-friendly design made for printing at home.

Can I share the file?

The game is for personal and classroom use only, so please don't share or resell the file.
