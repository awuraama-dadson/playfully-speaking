How to play

The Chief decides

No board, no apps. Just the cards and your loudest opinions.

1

Print & shuffle

Print the cards, cut them out and shuffle them into one pile. The oldest player is the first Chief. Respect your elders.

2

The Chief reads

The Chief draws a card and reads it out loud.

3

Everyone answers

Going round the circle, say what you'd do, argue your side or tell your story. Keep it short and sweet.

4

Best answer wins

The Chief gives the card to the best answer, then the player on their left becomes Chief. First to the target wins.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Will anything be shipped to me?

No. Chale, You Dey? is a digital download. You get instant access to the print-ready PDFs after purchase.

How do you win?

Every card you win is a point. First to 7 cards wins with 2 to 4 players, 5 cards with 5 players, and 4 cards with 6 to 8 players. If there's a tie, the tied players answer one more card and everyone else votes.

What if I've never lived that situation?

No stress. Make something up. The best invented answer counts just as much, and the Chief never has to explain their decision. Their court, their rules.

Can we play with just two people?

Yes. The Chief reads, the other player answers, and the Chief decides if the answer is good enough to win the card. Then swap.

Can I skip a card?

Yes. Just say “Chale, I pass.”

Who is it for?

Friend hangouts, family gatherings, December homecomings, couples and Ghanaians abroad. It's made for ages 16 and up.

What do I need to play?

Just the cards. Print them at 100% size and cut them out. A pen and paper to keep score is optional.

Can I share the file?

The game is for personal use only, so please don't share or resell the file.
