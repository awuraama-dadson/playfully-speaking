Good to know

Questions, answered

Will anything be shipped to me?

No. After Hours is a digital download. You get the printable files right after purchase and print them at home.

What do I need to play?

Just a printer, paper and scissors. No props, no apps and no internet connection.

Is it explicit?

No. The tone is playful, romantic and mildly flirty. A Little Heat is suggestive but never explicit, and you can skip any card without giving a reason.

We've been together for years. Is it still for us?

Yes. It works at any stage, from a third date to a tenth anniversary. Get to Know You, Again is written especially for long-term couples.

Can we play long-distance?

Absolutely. It works for long-distance video dates as well as nights in, anniversaries, Valentine's Day and birthdays.

Does it make a good gift?

It does. Print it, cut the cards, and wrap it up with the date night planner for a thoughtful gift.
